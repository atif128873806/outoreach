## WEEK 2 — War stories (all true)

### Post 4 (Mon) — The deliverability war story
**Visual:** your mail-tester score screenshot, or the DNS checker showing ✓✓✓.

> My product's emails were landing in spam. The fix took 3 DNS records and
> taught me more about email than 5 years of sending it.
>
> What I learned the hard way:
>
> 1. SPF, DKIM, DMARC aren't optional anymore. Gmail treats mail from
>    unauthenticated domains as suspicious by default. No records = spam
>    folder, no matter how good your email is.
> 2. The records are free and take ~20 minutes. Most people just don't know
>    they exist.
> 3. One gotcha that cost me hours: if your password has a $ in it, some
>    tools silently mangle it. Check the boring things first.
>
> I ended up building the checker I wished I had — free, no signup:
> outreach.sakodev.com/tools/dns-checker
>
> Check your domain. If you see a red ✗, that's where your emails are dying.

> What I did next:

> your product. Read acceptable-use policies BEFORE you build your checkout.
