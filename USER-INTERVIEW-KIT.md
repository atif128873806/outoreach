# User Feedback Kit — emails to your first 19 users

Goal: understand what's working, what's stuck, and why — not to pitch. Send
these **one at a time, personally** (not a BCC blast — people can tell, and
reply rates collapse). Use their first name. Send from your own address
(atif@…), plain text, no logos or marketing design — this is a person writing
to a person.

**How to pick which email:** use your `/admin` activation funnel.
- Sent real emails / running campaigns → **Email A**
- Found leads or set up, but never sent → **Email B**
- Signed up and went quiet early → **Email C**
- Not sure / want one for everyone → **Email D**

Golden rule for replies & calls: ask about what they **did** and where they got
**stuck** — never "what features do you want." Frustrations tell you what to
build; wishlists lie.

## If they say yes to a call — the 15-minute guide

Don't present or defend. Listen 80% of the time. Questions in order:

1. **Warm-up / context:** "Before Outreach Studio, how were you finding clients
   / doing outreach?" (You're learning their world and their alternatives.)
2. **The trigger:** "What made you look for a tool like this?"
3. **First run:** "Walk me through what you did the first time you logged in —
   click by click if you can." (This is where you *see* the friction, not hear
   about it.)
4. **The stuck point:** "Where did you slow down or get confused?" Then stay
   silent — let them fill it.
5. **Reality check:** "What did you expect to happen that didn't?"
6. **The alternative:** "When it didn't do what you needed, what did you do
   instead?" (Their workaround = your next feature.)
7. **The value moment (if any):** "Was there a point where it clearly saved you
   time or did something useful? What was it?"
8. **Only at the very end:** "If you had a magic wand, what's the one thing
   you'd change?" — then dig underneath any answer: "what were you trying to do
   when you needed that?"

**During the call:** when they criticize something, your only reply is "tell me
more about that." Never explain why they're wrong. Write down their exact words
— the phrases they use are your future marketing copy.

**After every call:** jot the top 1–2 things you heard. After 5–6 calls, the
pattern will be obvious — and *that's* your roadmap, written by the people
paying (or about to pay) for it.

## Logistics that matter

- Send individually, spaced out (5–10 a day), not all at once.
- One gentle follow-up after ~4 days to non-repliers: "just floating this back
  up in case it got buried — even one line helps." Then stop.
- Expect ~30–50% to reply to a personal founder email at this size. A handful
  of real conversations is a huge win.
- Keep a simple note (even a text file) of what each person says. Patterns >
  individual opinions.
