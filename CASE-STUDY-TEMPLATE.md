# Case Study Kit — how to produce your first real case study

A case study is your strongest sales asset — but only if every number in it is
real. **Never publish invented results**; one caught fake kills the brand.
This kit: (1) how to generate the data, (2) what to capture, (3) the fill-in
template, (4) where to publish it.

## 1. Generate the data (the dogfood campaign IS the case study)

Run your own outreach campaign selling Outreach Studio (after the separate
cold-outreach domain is set up — see ROADMAP):

- Lead Finder → 100–150 web/marketing agencies or freelancers
- Campaign brief: the honest meta-pitch ("this email was found, written, and
  sent by the tool I'm selling")
- Warm-up ON, throttle 10–15/hour, 2 follow-ups, A/B subjects ON
- Let it run 2–3 weeks minimum before writing anything

Alternative source: your **first happy customer**. Offer them 3 months of Pro
free in exchange for their numbers + a quote + their name/logo. One real
customer story beats your own dogfood story.

## 2. Capture these numbers (all visible in the app)

From the campaign page + dashboard, screenshot AND write down:

| Metric | Where |
|---|---|
| Leads found (and % with email) | Lead Finder result meta |
| Emails sent / delivery failures | Campaign log |
| Open rate | Campaign stats |
| Click rate (if link used) | Campaign stats |
| Reply rate + classification split | Replies tab |
| A/B arms: open rate per subject style | Campaign page |
| Positive outcomes: calls booked, deals, $ value | your notes — track manually! |
| Time spent (setup + weekly maintenance) | your notes |

Screenshots to keep: campaign stats card, the 14-day dashboard chart, one
(anonymized) generated email, one real positive reply (get permission or blur).

## 4. Where to publish

1. Page on the site (`/blog/first-case-study` — becomes the anchor for all ads
   and posts)
2. LinkedIn: the Week-3 numbers post (CONTENT-CALENDAR.md Post 7/10 format)
   linking to the full study
3. X thread: one metric per tweet, chart screenshot, link at the end
4. Reddit r/EntrepreneurRideAlong / indie communities: full transparency
   version including what went wrong — those communities reward honesty and
   punish marketing-speak
5. Directory listings: several let you attach "results" or blog links — add it
