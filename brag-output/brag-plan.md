# Brag Plan: Outreach Studio

## What is this app?
Outreach Studio finds business leads with their email addresses attached, has AI write a genuinely personal cold email to each one, sends them from the user's own mailbox at a human pace, and triages the replies — the whole client-acquisition pipeline in one product.

## The angle
Four words, four screens: **Find. Write. Send. Listen.** The video is the pipeline itself, shown in real UI, in order. No mascots, no abstract 3D — the proof is that each screen is a real product surface with real copy on it. The closing claim is the one no competitor makes: *"Every email different. Every one from you."*

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A single search field on white types **"dentists" · "Austin, TX"** and hits Find. That's the hook: the viewer instantly understands the input, and wants to see the output.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The leads land** — three result rows arrive one by one, each with a green `✉ email found` badge; a small counter reads *18 found · 14 with email*.
- **The AI writes it** — an email body types itself in, with the personalized phrases highlighted in brand blue; then a green `✓ 0 spam flags` chip snaps in.
- **It sends itself, safely** — a campaign progress bar fills to 62% with `12/hour` and a `09:00–17:00` send window visible; a follow-up row flips to `stopped — replied ✓`.
- **A reply comes back, already sorted** — an `● interested` pill, a real reply line, and an *"✨ Suggested reply"* card underneath.

## Outro / punchline
Three KPI tiles snap in — **51% open · 13% reply · 0 templates used** — then the logo mark draws itself, wordmark, and the line: *"Every email different. Every one from you."* Then the URL.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, exactly as a real user experiences it:
1. **Entry:** type a niche + city into Lead Finder → real businesses with emails come back.
2. **Key action:** describe the offer once → AI writes a different, personalized email per contact → spam-checked.
3. **Result:** campaign sends at a throttled pace inside business hours → replies arrive classified with an AI-drafted answer ready.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film — a real tool doing real work, no hype
- Interpretation: restraint is the point. Four beats, slow confident reveals, generous whitespace, mixed-case medium-weight type, crossfades not slams. Motion exists to direct the eye to product truth, never to impress on its own. The product's own UI colors carry all the personality needed.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22s target (four product beats + outro)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f7f7f8` (app canvas), pure `#ffffff` for UI cards
- Accent: brand gradient `#2563eb` → `#7c3aed` (logo, primary buttons, highlights)
- Text: `#18181b` primary, `#71717a` secondary, `#a1a1aa` muted
- Support colors from the real UI: emerald `#0e9f6e` (email found / verified / opens), amber `#d97706` (replies / warnings), dark `#09090b` (logo tile, CTA sections)
- Display font: Geist (Geist Sans) — the product's actual font
- Body font: Geist; `Geist Mono` for data/badges
- Strongest visual element: the Lead Finder result row — business name + green email badge + tech chips — because it is unmistakably *this* product and nothing else.

## Share copy (draft)
Type "dentists in Austin" → get real businesses with emails → AI writes a different email for every one → sends from your mailbox at a human pace. Outreach Studio, free to start.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm professional bed with sparse, motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM, clean corporate-optimistic; supports a 22s cut with a natural early lift)
- Music treatment: start at 0, bed volume ~0.22 under the whole film, gentle 0.4s fade-in, fade out over the final 1.2s so the closing line lands in near-silence
- Music cue guidance: preset cue file read. Strong cues to hang the three big reveals on: **4.23s** (leads land), **10.54s** (spam-check chip / AI proof), **16.34s** (KPI tiles). Beat grid for sequential events (~0.53s spacing): use `5.28 / 5.80 / 6.34` for the three lead rows arriving one by one. Timing hints only — readability and product clarity win over the grid.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — nothing pulses or bounces; at most a barely-perceptible lift in accent brightness on the three strong cues
- SFX posture: sparse and professional. Roughly five total: soft keypress cluster for the typed query, one light `click` on Find, three quiet `switch`/`drop` ticks as lead rows arrive, one dry `bong`/`select` on the "0 spam flags" chip, one soft accent on the reply pill. Nothing on the logo — silence is the outro.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed search query (keypresses), Find click, lead rows arriving one by one, spam-flag chip snap, KPI tiles snapping in
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no risers, no impacts on text, no music ducking gymnastics. If an SFX doesn't correspond to a real interaction on screen, it doesn't exist. Total SFX must never read as a "video edit" — it should read as a screen recording of software.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: the query — 3.5s
White canvas, generous margins. A single Lead Finder search row: two input fields and a dark `Find leads` button. `dentists` types into the niche field, then `Austin, TX` into the location field, then the button gets a click press-state. Small muted label above: `Lead Finder`. Use the real field labels from the product ("Niche", "Location").
Sequential/interaction: yes — text types character-grouped into field 1, then field 2, then a cursor click on the Find button (press + release scale).
Audio intent: quiet, precise, human — someone actually typing. Music has just entered, low.
Audio-coupled idea: keypress cluster during typing; single soft click on the button.
Music: warm bed entering at low volume.
Transition mood: clean (0.5s crossfade) → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The leads land — 5s
The results card. Header line reads `18 found · 14 with email · 11 with Instagram` (real product meta copy). Three result rows arrive one by one: **Bright Smile Dental** / **Lakeway Family Dentistry** / **Austin Ortho Group** — each with a muted `Dentist · Austin` sub-line and a green `✉ info@brightsmile.com`-style badge; one row also shows a `★ owner found` chip and one a `WordPress` chip. Nothing else moves.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three rows arrive one by one (~0.5s apart, on the beat grid), each fading up 8px.
Audio intent: satisfying accumulation — the sense of a list filling with real contacts.
Audio-coupled idea: one quiet tick per row arrival, aligned to `5.28 / 5.80 / 6.34`; the first row lands on strong cue `4.23s` if timing allows.
Music: bed lifts naturally into its first strong beat.
Transition mood: clean (0.6s crossfade) → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The AI writes it — 5.5s
An email preview card. Subject line at top: `Fewer no-shows for Bright Smile`. Body types in, two short paragraphs, with two phrases highlighted in a soft blue tint — `dental practices in Austin` and `you handle family and cosmetic work` — so the viewer *sees* the personalization. Sign-off `— Atif, SakoDev`. Then a green `✓ 0 spam-filter flags` chip and a muted `Written by AI · 96 words` chip snap in beneath.
Sequential/interaction: yes — body text reveals in reading order (line-grouped, not per character), then the two chips snap in last.
Audio intent: calm competence. The chip is the only punctuation.
Audio-coupled idea: soft typing texture under the body reveal; one dry select/bong tick exactly on the spam-flag chip at strong cue `10.54s`.
Music: steady bed, no lift needed — the visual is doing the work.
Transition mood: clean (0.6s crossfade) → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Sends safely, replies come back sorted — 5s
Split beat on one card. Top half: campaign row `Austin dentists — March push` with a `● running` pill, a progress bar filling to 62%, and two muted stats `112 of 180 sent` / `12 / hour`; a small `Send window 09:00–17:00` line. A follow-up row beneath flips from `due in 3 days` to `stopped — replied ✓` in emerald. Bottom half: a reply card — `● interested` pill, `Bright Smile Dental · 4 min ago`, the line *"This is actually something we've been meaning to fix. Do you have time Thursday?"*, and an `✨ Suggested reply` card under it.
Sequential/interaction: yes — progress bar fills, then the follow-up row flips state, then the reply card fades up last.
Audio intent: resolution — the machine worked and a human answered.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft accent as the reply pill appears; nothing on the progress bar (it's ambient).
Music: bed continues, moving toward its resolve.
Transition mood: soft (0.7s crossfade) → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Numbers, then the name — 3s
Three KPI tiles snap in on the strong cue: `51% open rate` · `13% reply rate` · `0 templates used`. They hold, then crossfade to the closing card: the logo mark (open ring with the escaping message dot, brand gradient) draws/scales in on the dark `#09090b` tile, `Outreach Studio` wordmark beside it, the line **"Every email different. Every one from you."** beneath, and `outreach.sakodev.com` last, small and muted.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three tiles arrive together-but-staggered (~0.12s), then logo, then wordmark, then tagline, then URL.
Audio intent: the tiles land on the beat; then the music fades and the last frame is nearly silent — restraint as the final statement.
Audio-coupled idea: KPI tiles on strong cue `16.34s`; deliberately **no** sound on the logo.
Music: final lift on the tiles, then fade out across the last 1.2s.
Transition mood: end card holds ~1.2s in near-silence.

## Delivery expectations
- `brag-output/brag.mp4` — 1920x1080, ~22s, H.264, music + sparse SFX baked in.
- `brag-output/brag.jpg` — best frame chosen from Scene 3 (the AI-written email with highlighted personalization) or Scene 2 (leads with email badges); whichever reads most clearly as a thumbnail at small size. Baked as frame 0.
- `brag-output/share-copy.txt` — LinkedIn-first caption plus a shorter X variant.
- Must be legible when muted and when scaled to ~640px wide (LinkedIn feed reality).
