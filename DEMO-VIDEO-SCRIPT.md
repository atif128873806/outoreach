# Demo Video Kit — scripts, shot list, and recording guide

Two videos, in priority order:
- **A. 90-second launch cut** — for the landing page, Product Hunt gallery,
  and social. Most people only ever watch this one.
- **B. 4-minute walkthrough** — for /docs and YouTube ("Outreach Studio
  tutorial"), where search traffic finds it.

## Recording setup (free)

- **Screen recorder:** OBS Studio (Linux/Win/Mac) — 1920×1080, 30fps.
  Simpler alternative: Loom (browser, free tier, auto-hosted link).
- **Mic:** any headset beats laptop mic. Record in a quiet room, keep the mic
  10–15 cm away. Don't worry about your accent — clarity and energy matter,
  a founder's real voice outperforms polished voiceover.
- **Browser prep:** clean profile, 100–125% zoom, hide bookmarks bar, close
  other tabs. Log into a **demo account with realistic data already loaded**
  (run a Lahore/Austin lead search beforehand, have one campaign with sent
  stats, a couple of replies in the Message Center).
- **Cursor discipline:** move slowly, pause on what you're naming. Every cut:
  action first, then talk about it.
- Record narration WHILE clicking; re-record a scene if you stumble — scenes
  are short on purpose.

## A. The 90-second launch cut

| # | Screen (what you record) | Narration (say this) | ~sec |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Landing page, slow scroll of hero | "This is Outreach Studio — it finds business leads, writes a personal email to each one, and follows up until they reply." | 0–8 |
| 2 | Lead Finder: type "dentists" + "Austin, TX", click Find, show progress stages, results appear with email badges | "Type a niche and a city. It searches the web, visits every website, and comes back with real businesses — emails included." | 8–25 |
| 3 | Click "Import all → Contacts" | "One click, and they're contacts." | 25–30 |
| 4 | New campaign: type rough one-line idea, click ✨ Improve with AI, brief appears | "Describe your offer in one line — the AI sharpens it into a brief." | 30–42 |
| 5 | Click Preview: generated email appears; point cursor at the personalized first line, then the "0 spam flags" badge | "Then it writes a different email for every contact. Look — no templates, no {{name}} brackets. It even checks it against spam filters before anything sends." | 42–60 |
| 6 | Campaign page: progress bar, throttle, send window; hover follow-up row "stopped — replied" | "Emails go out at a human pace, inside business hours, with follow-ups that stop the moment someone replies." | 60–72 |
| 7 | Message Center replies tab: interested reply + AI suggested answer, click Copy | "Replies come back classified — and it drafts your answer." | 72–82 |
| 8 | Pricing page, cursor on Free card → cut to logo + URL on white | "Free plan, AI included, no card. outreach-dot-sakodev-dot-com." | 82–90 |

**Editing notes:** cut every pause; add a subtle zoom on the generated email
(scene 5) — it's the money shot; background music at −20dB or none; end card
= logo + URL for 3 full seconds.

## B. The 4-minute walkthrough (YouTube: "How to send AI-personalized cold email — Outreach Studio tutorial")

Structure — each chapter is one recorded scene; timestamps become YouTube
chapters:

1. **0:00 Intro (15s)** — camera or screen: "In four minutes: from an empty
   account to a running outreach campaign. Nothing pre-made, watch the clock."
2. **0:15 Settings (40s)** — fill sender identity ("this is what the AI
   writes as — 2 minutes here is worth it"), show SMTP fields + test email
   button, mention simulation mode: "no mailbox yet? Everything still works —
   sends are simulated."
3. **0:55 Lead Finder (50s)** — run a real search live, talk over the
   progress stages ("it's visiting each website right now, pulling emails,
   Instagram, LinkedIn"), point out company-intel notes, click Find owner on
   one lead, import all.
4. **1:45 Campaign (60s)** — rough brief → ✨ improve → preview → read the
   generated email out loud, point at the personalization ("it used what the
   business actually does — different for every contact") → spam check →
   follow-ups + A/B + send window → schedule.
5. **2:45 What sending looks like (30s)** — campaign page log filling in,
   caps/warm-up ("this slow pace is a feature — it's what keeps you out of
   spam").
6. **3:15 Replies (30s)** — classified replies, AI-suggested answer, bounce
   auto-handling.
7. **3:45 Close (15s)** — pricing glance: "Free to start, $9 and $29 when you
   scale. Link below. If you get stuck, the docs cover everything."

**YouTube description:** first two lines = value + link; then chapter
timestamps; then the /docs, /tools/spam-checker and /tools/dns-checker links
(each is a search magnet).

## After recording

- Landing page: put the 90s cut in the hero area (Phase-2 task: upload as
  static MP4 in /public or embed YouTube unlisted — YouTube is zero-cost CDN).
- Product Hunt gallery: PH accepts video first slide — use the 90s cut.
- /docs: embed the 4-minute walkthrough at the top of Quick start.
- Repurpose: cut scenes 2, 5, 7 of video A as three vertical 15–20s clips for
  LinkedIn/X posts (screen-recording + caption, no voice needed).
